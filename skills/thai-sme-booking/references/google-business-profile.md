# Google Business Profile (Google Maps)

Part of the Complete package. For local appointment businesses, Maps is often the #1 source of new customers.

## Steps (with the owner, using the shop's Google account)
1. https://business.google.com → Add business → exact shop name (same as the site and LINE OA) → category (e.g. ร้านทำผม / Hair salon, สปา, คลินิกความงาม).
2. Location: a pin on the map + address. Service-area-only businesses (home visits) hide the address.
3. Phone + website URL (the Pages site). Booking link: the LIFF URL `https://liff.line.me/<LIFF ID>` (a "Book" link where offered, or in the description).
4. **Verify**: Google chooses the method (video recording of the shop, phone/SMS, email, or postcard). A video usually shows the signboard, the interior, and proof of management (e.g. unlocking the door or the cash register). Plan for the owner to do this themselves.
5. Hours (must match the Hours tab), special hours for holidays.
6. Photos: at least 3 exterior (with signage), 3 interior, 5 work samples, 1 logo. Real photos, no stock.
7. Services + prices (mirror the Services tab), description (750 chars, Thai with key services and the area name).
8. Reviews: create the "Ask for reviews" short link → put it in Settings `google_review_url` (shows on `/reviews`) and add an FAQ row, e.g. keywords `รีวิว` → the link. Owners can also send it to happy customers in chat.

## Ongoing (Care+ idea)
- Reply to every review within a few days (thank positive ones; take negative ones offline politely).
- Post a monthly update/offer, and update holiday hours.
- Check Insights (calls, direction requests, website clicks) and add them to the monthly report.

## Don'ts
- Don't add keywords to the business name ("บ้านสวย ซาลอน ทำผม ย้อมสี ราคาถูก"), which can get the listing suspended.
- Don't buy or fake reviews, and don't offer discounts in exchange for 5 stars (against Google policy).
